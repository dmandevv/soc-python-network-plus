Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP):
- detailed plan for resuming operations after a disaster 
- application, data center, building, campus, region

Extensive planning:
- backups
- off-site data replication
- cloud alternatives
- remote-site (hot/warm/cold)

Third party options:
- physical alternate location
- recovery services

Recovery Time Objective:
- time to resume operations
- get up and running quickly, then back to a set service level in this timeframe

Recovery Point Objective:
- how much time of data loss is acceptable
Banking transactions (less than 1 hour)
Web site updates (1-4 hours)

Mean time to repair (MTTR):
- average time to fix the issue
- full time from point of failure to full functionality

Mean time between failures (MTBF):
- predict the time between failures
- use past failures to set this value

Site resiliency:
1. Recovery site is prepped (data is synchronized)
2. Business failovers to alternate site
3. Problem is addressed
4. Revert to primary location

Cold/Warm/Hot sites

Cold site:
no hardware, empty building
no data, need to bring it with you
no people, bus them in 

Warm site:
in the middle
usually hardware is ready, just need people and data

Hot site:
near exact replica 
stocked with hardware (but two of everything, one for normal site, other for hot site)
software and applications are running and up-to-date (automated process)
quick to transfer over (minutes, not hours)

Tabletop exercises:
sit at a table and discuss who would do what in event of disaster
cheaper than full-scale disaster drill

Validation tests:
use well defined rules of engagement
do not touch production
very specific scenarios
evaluate response (document and discuss)
