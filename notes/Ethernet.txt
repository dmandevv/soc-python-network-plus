IEEE 802.3 committee maintains Ethernet standards

1000Base-T - Gigabit ethernet - Copper - 1GBit/s
10GBase-T - 10 Gigabit ethernet - Copper - 10GBit/s
10GBase-SX - Gigabit ethernet - Fiber - 1GBit/s

T is twisted copper pair
F is Fiber
SX would be short wavelength light

Fiber Communication:
No RF signal - harder to tap
signal slow to degrade
immune to radio interferance

Multimode Fiber:
short range (2km)
cheap, LED

Single-mode Fiber:
long range (100km)
expensive, lasers

Twisted Pair Copper Cable:
balanced pair
two wires with equal and opposite signals
Transmit+/Transmit- and Receive+/Receive-
wires twist away from interferance
opposite signals are compared on other end

Coaxial Cable:
two or more forms share a common axis
RG-6 for tv/Cable
internet over Cable

Twinaxial Cable:
two innder conducators (twinax)
common on 10Gbit ethernet
short distance, low cost

Plenum-rated Cable:
PVC or FEP jacket
