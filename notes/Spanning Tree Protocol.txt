Loop protection:
- connect two switches with cables forming a Loop
- will send traffic back and forth forever
- no counting mechanism at the MAC layer
- network will crash
- IEEE 802.1D prevents loops in bridged (Switch) networks

Spanning Tree Protocol 802.1D (STP)

STP port states
- Blocking
    - not forwarding to prevent a loop
- Listening
    - not forwarding and cleaning the MAC table
- Learning
    - not forwarding and adding to the MAC table
- Forwarding
    - data passes through
- Disabled
    - Admin has turned off port

Rapid Spanning Tree Protocol 802.1W (RSTP)

- current standard, upgrade from STP
- faster convergence, from 30-50 seconds down to 6 seconds
- backwards compatible with 802.1D STP
