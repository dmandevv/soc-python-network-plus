Power over Ethernet (PoE):
- one wire for both network and electricity

    Power provided at the switch - Endspan
    In-line power injector - Midspan

    PoE - 15.4 watts DC power, 350 mA max current
    PoE+ - 25.5 watts DC power, 600 mA max current
    PoE++ - Type 3 - 51 watts DC power, 600 mA max current
          - Type 4 - 71.3 watts DC power, 960 mA max current
          - more ethernet standards available
    Compare device with switch support: PoE+ switch can't power a PoE++ device

    PoE Switch:
    - marked on the switch or interfaces
    - check for PoE power supported, don't exceed capacity


Transceivers:
- have to match the fiber (single mode or multimode)
- check wavelength (850nm, 1310nm)
- if misconfigured, signal issues
- check devices sensitivity level (how much signal is needed to understand)
- calculate the power budget:
    - transmitter power (dBm) - decibals per milliwatt
    - signal loss based on distance
total power budget = transmitter power - signal loss
compare to the sensitivity level
    - higher? we have enough power
    - lower? not enough power
