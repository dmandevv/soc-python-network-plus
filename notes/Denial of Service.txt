Force a service to fail

1. overload with packets
2. take advantage of exploit (why patching is important)
3. make system unavailable
4. create a smokescreen for other exploit (precursor to a DNS spoofing attack)
5. could be as simple as turning off power switch

"Friendly" DoS
- network DoS - layer 2 loop without Spanning Tree Protocol (STP)
- bandwidth DoS - downloading a multi-gigabyte Linus distro over DSL
- water line break over servers

DDoS:
- botnets coordinating an attack
- asymmetric threat - attacker with much smaller resources can attack large companies

DDoS reflection/amplification:
- send small amount of info to a server while spoofing targets IP - receive large response
- multiplied by 1000s of zombie hosts, target is overwhelmed
- easy to do using Protocols with minimal authentication checks (NTP, DNS, ICMP)
