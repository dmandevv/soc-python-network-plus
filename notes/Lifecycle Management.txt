End of Life (EOL):
- manufacturer stops making product
- may continure to provide security patches
- warrant repair

End of Service/Support (EOS):
- manufacturer stops updating product
- no more security updates

Service Packs: contain large amount of updates bundled
Monthly updates: incremental
Emergency updates: zero day

Firmware Management:
- software inside of hardware
- always have a rollback plan (save Firmware binaries)

Decommissioning:
- asset disposal
- sanitize media or destroy device
- may have legal issues
    - some information cannot be destroyed (legal hold)
    - consider offsite storage
- do NOT throw in dumpster

Change Management:
- how to make a Change
- have clear policies: frequency, duration, installation process, fallback prodecures

Request Process Tracking:
help desk
- take call, triage, determine next step, assign ticket and monitor
