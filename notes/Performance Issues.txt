1000BASE-T transfers 1Gbit/s
busy network might try to send 2Gbits/s, packets get queued and buffered

Bottlenecks:
I/O bus, CPU speed, storage access speed, WAN bandwidth, local network speed
must monitor ALL of them to find the slow ones

Latency:
- delay between request and response
- examine at every step to see any spikes
- can require many measurement tools, but worth the payoff in the accuracy

Packet Loss:
- discards
- no errors in packet, but system could not transmit or receive data
- corrupted during transmission, dropped after validation, needs to be resent

Jitter:
- real-time data is sensitive to delay
- data should arrive at regular intervals
- voice and audio stuttering
- jitter is the time between frames, try to keep it small and consistent
