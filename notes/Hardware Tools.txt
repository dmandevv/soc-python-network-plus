Tone Generator:
- where does that wire go? Follow the Tone
- outs an analog sound on wire
- then use an Inductive Probe to listen to wire
- connect the tone generator to the wire:
    modular jack, coax, punch down connector
- use Probe to locate sound

Cable Tester:
- check if pins are connected/disconnected
- crossed wires
- verifies punchdown done properly

Taps and port mirrors:
- intercept network traffic
- Physical taps: disconnect the link, put tap in middle
    - can be active or passive (power)
- Port Mirror (SPAN)
    - software based
    - bandwidth limited

Wireless Survery Tool:
- signal coverage, potential interferance, built in or 3rd party

Wi-Fi Analyzer:
- hardware based
- view all 802.11 info in the air
- Spectrum analyzer can check frequencies

Visual Fault Locator:
- flashlight for optical fiber
- shine light down fiber, can see light bleeding from broken fiber jacket
