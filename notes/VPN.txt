Concentrator:
- encryption/decryption access device
- often part of firewall

- VPNs can be specialized cryptographic hardware or software-based

Client-to-site VPN:
- on-demand accress from a remote device
- connects to VPN Concentrator
- can be configured to be "always-on"

Site-to-site VPN:
- always-on
- Firewalls act as VPN Concentrators

Clientless VPN:
- HTML5
- API support (web cryptograpy API)
- just need to use an HTML5 compliant browser


Full Tunnel:
- ALL traffic sent through VPN Tunnel

Split Tunnel:
- VPN traffic sent through Tunnel
- Non-VPN traffic (non work related, non confidential) is sent normally
