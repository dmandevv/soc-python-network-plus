# Network+ (N10-009)

Step 3 of my [SOC Python Journey](https://github.com/dmandevv/soc-python-journey).

Studying CompTIA Network+ (N10-009), building the networking foundation for Security+ and SOC analyst work.

## Approach

**Working through every term in the official CompTIA objectives document, one at a time**, and writing an explanation of each into the file for its objective. Depth over coverage — a term is done when the mechanism is clear, not when it has been defined.

Each completed objective gets a **20-question quiz** with plausible distractors, and every miss is worked through rather than simply corrected.

**Alongside it runs a hands-on practice track** — roughly three to four hours a day across free platforms, on the principle that learning by doing beats reading definitions, and that the exam should come well after readiness rather than to a schedule.
